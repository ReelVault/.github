# ReelVault

**ReelVault is a self-hosted media server.** Run it at home, point it at your movies and TV shows, and watch them on any screen in the house. Posters, subtitles and resume come included. Your files and watch history stay on your own hardware.

## Get started

- **Install:** with Docker or a one-line installer. See [Install & run](https://reelvault.org/guide/getting-started).
- **Learn:** [What is ReelVault?](https://reelvault.org/guide/introduction) and the [FAQ](https://reelvault.org/guide/faq).
- **Build:** the [SDK](https://reelvault.org/sdk/) and the [plugin guide](https://reelvault.org/plugins/getting-started).
